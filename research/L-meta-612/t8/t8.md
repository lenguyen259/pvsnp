# L-meta-612, route T8: a dead-gate Reader Lemma

Status: **NOT closed.** New results:
* **PROVED: Lemma DG.** This is an exact identity for the gates that rule R removes, dead gates included, for any
  restriction of type A, N or B (in fact for any x_X := 0, or x_S := 1 with S independent).
* **PROVED: Theorem DV2.** It gives a structural lower bound for A(c), with a C4 injectivity step. Under explicit
  local hypotheses it shows that A(c) is paid by syntactic eliminations alone. **Corollary SL**: in every reduced
  circuit of AND-depth 1, A(c) is paid at every star centre. This explains the T2-LT counterexample of t7
  (Petersen at size Phi is such a circuit).
* **DATA.** The DV2 certificate holds at some vertex in 18,378 of the 18,535 pool circuits (99.2%), with no
  violation of any proved inequality. **Every triangle-free circuit of size <= Phi that was tested has a certified
  vertex**: all 5,053 such circuits in the t2 cache, plus size-Phi fuzzing on 11 graphs including Petersen. If this
  were proved in the form "no certified vertex implies size >= Phi+1", it would close girth >= 5 (§3b, §5).
* **REFUTED: T2-LD** (some option is paid by constants, copies and dead gates alone, with no Top merge). Smallest
  counterexample: net, size Phi+2. It holds on all 70,452 cache circuits.
* **REFUTED: "every reduced circuit has a DV2-certified vertex".** Smallest counterexamples: P4 at Phi+1 and the bull
  at Phi (a triangle graph).
* **REFUTED: "some star centre has A(c) LD-paid" in STAR-ALL circuits.** Smallest counterexample: a 6-vertex triangle
  graph at Phi+1. It also fails on C5{012} and n8 at Phi+3.
* **REFUTED: "k_or(c) = 0 implies A(c) is paid".** Counterexample: net, Phi+1.
* **Not proved:** T2-LR for subcubic graphs or for general C4-free graphs. The obstruction that remains is described
  exactly in §5.

## 1. Lemma DG (PROVED, any graph, any reduced C)

Let rho be x_X := 0 (with f|rho ≠ 0) or x_S := 1 with S independent. Write x_rho for the restricted variables.
* K = gates that are constant under rho. Then K = D_X (gates <= OR_X) for a 0-option, and K = R_S for a 1-option.
* Cp = gates outside K with an input in K ∪ x_rho. These are the OR gates of ∂D_X, or the AND gates of ∂R_S.
* **Z** = gates all of whose paths to the output meet K (so K ⊆ Z). Dead = Z \ (K ∪ Cp). L = Cp \ Z (the *live copies*).
* I = nodes outside Z ∪ x_rho that feed a gate of Z. arcs_I = number of arcs from I into Z. F_rho = number of arcs
  leaving x_rho. r(p) = number of readers of p.

**Lemma DG.** Every gate of Z ∪ L is eliminated by rule R, and
  **LD(rho) = |Z ∪ L| = F_rho + arcs_I + Σ_{z∈Z} (r(z) − 1).**
In particular LD(rho) >= F_rho + |I|.

*Proof.*
(i) K is the set of syntactic constants. For a 0-option, an AND gate a ∧ b lies in D_X iff a or b does. This holds
because two nonzero monotone functions are both 1 at 1_{V\X}. An OR gate lies in D_X iff both inputs do. The case
x_S := 1 is dual. Rule R therefore makes K constant and makes every Cp gate a copy of its other input.
(ii) A gate that survives rule R reaches the output in the rewritten circuit. That path lifts to an original path
whose interior gates are copies, so it avoids K. Hence survivors lie outside Z, and Z ∪ L is eliminated.
(iii) Every reader of a node of Z lies in Z ∪ L. A reader of a K-gate is in K or in Cp. If z ∈ Z \ K, then every
path through a reader q of z meets K after z, so q ∈ Z. Readers of x_rho lie in K ∪ Cp. The output is not in Z,
because f|rho is not constant.
(iv) An L-gate has exactly one input in K ∪ x_rho (two inputs there would put it in K). Its other input is not in Z,
by (iii).
(v) Count the arcs entering Z: 2|Z| = arcs(x_rho→Z) + arcs(Z→Z) + arcs_I. By (iii) and (iv),
Σ_Z r(z) = arcs(Z→Z) + arcs(Z→L), and arcs(Z→L) = |L| − arcs(x_rho→L). Also
arcs(x_rho→Z) + arcs(x_rho→L) = F_rho. Substituting gives 2|Z| + |L| = F_rho + arcs_I + Σ_Z r(z). ∎

Check (`a4.py`): the identity holds exactly on all 442,989 (circuit, option) pairs of the pools (t3 locpool, t5 pool,
t5 pool2, and the two t7 counterexamples), with 0 failures.

Reading. The first-order Reader Lemma (t2) is the term F_rho plus the part of arcs_I that enters K. **Dead gates add
exactly the arcs that enter Z through private cones, plus the multiple readers inside Z.** A star x_c ∧ OR_B with a
private OR-tree contributes |B| arcs, which is the t7 mechanism. If a cone is shared, its maximal shared gate y
enters as **one** arc in place of |sing(y)| arcs. That is the only loss.

## 2. Theorem DV2: the A option at a vertex c (PROVED, any C4-free G, reduced C)

Fix c such that G − c has an edge, and let rho = (x_c := 0), with K = D_c, Z = Z_c and L = L_c. Then:
* k_Z = number of readers of x_c inside Z (all AND readers, and the dead OR readers);
* k_L = number of live OR readers p_j = x_c ∨ z_j;
* the **cut sources** are the nodes of K ∪ {x_c} that have an L-reader;
* N_D(c) = {w ∈ N(c) : some cut source s ∈ K has s(e_c + e_w) = 1};
* for a gate y ∈ I, S_c(y) = {w : y(e_c + e_w) = 1, y(e_c) = 0}, and pen(c) = Σ_{y ∈ I gate} max(0, |S_c(y) ∩ N(c)| − 1);
* p_j *carries* w if C evaluated at e_w with p_j forced to 1 outputs 1.

**Cut Lemma.** For every w ∈ N(c), some cut source s has s(e_c + e_w) = 1. If w ∉ N_D(c), then the circuit evaluated
at e_w with all live OR readers of x_c forced to 1 outputs 1.
*Proof.* Put α = e_c + e_w and β = e_w. Suppose every cut source is 0 at α. Show, in topological order, that every
node outside Z ∪ {x_c} takes the same value at α and at β. A gate outside Z ∪ L has no input in K ∪ x_c (it would be
in Cp, hence in L) and none in Z \ K (it would be in Z, by DG (iii)). An L-gate s ∨ t has s = 0 at both points and t
outside Z ∪ x_c. The output lies outside Z, so f(α) = f(β) = 0, which contradicts cw ∈ E. The second claim follows
from the same induction with the L-readers of x_c forced to 1. ∎

**Theorem DV2.**
(a) LD_A(c) >= k_Z + k_L + |N_D(c)| − pen(c).
(b) (C4 step) A live OR reader p = x_c ∨ z with sing(z) ≠ ∅ carries at most one vertex of N(c).
(c) Suppose every w ∈ N(c) \ N_D(c) is carried by a single live OR reader whose z has a singleton. Then
    LD_A(c) >= k_Z + deg c − pen(c). So **A(c) is paid as soon as pen(c) <= k_Z − δ_c**, where need_A(c) = deg c + δ_c.
    In particular this holds if k_Z >= 1 and no shared gate of I carries two neighbours of c.

*Proof.*
(a) Let w ∈ N_D(c), with a cut source s ∈ D_c such that s(α) = 1. Then s(e_c) = 0, since s <= x_c and s is not the
variable x_c. Now s is computed from x_c and from the nodes of I that lie below it inside Z. So some such node y
changes value between e_c and α. Either y = x_w, or y is a gate with w ∈ S_c(y). Hence
|N_D| <= |I_var| + |I_gate| + pen, and Lemma DG gives LD >= F_c + |I| >= k_Z + k_L + |N_D| − pen.
(b) Suppose p carries w_1 ≠ w_2 in N(c), and let u ∈ sing(z). Then z(e_c) = 0 (otherwise p = z), so u ≠ c. Also
z(e_{w_i}) = 0, because otherwise forcing p changes nothing and the output at e_{w_i} would be 1. At e_u + e_{w_i}
the gate p is 1 and every other node is >= its value at e_{w_i}. By monotonicity f(e_u + e_{w_i}) = 1, so
u w_i ∈ E for i = 1, 2. Then c w_1 u w_2 is a C4. ∎
(c) Choose a single carrier for each w ∈ N(c) \ N_D. By (b) this map is injective, so |N(c) \ N_D| <= k_L. Now apply (a). ∎

**Corollary SL (PROVED).** Let C be reduced, of AND-depth 1, for C4-free G. Then for every star centre c, A(c) is
paid by LD: LD_A(c) >= deg c + 1.
*Proof.* With nothing above an AND gate except ORs, every AND gate is <= f. So every rectangle lies inside E and
has no singleton. By C4-freeness every AND gate is a clean star x_c ∧ OR_B with B ⊆ N(c). Gates of an OR-tree are
never read above the AND layer (out >= x_b is impossible), and a tree gate OR_S with |S| >= 2 serves only one
centre (S ⊆ N(c) ∩ N(c') gives a C4). Hence Z_c contains the c-stars and their trees. I consists of variables only,
so pen = 0. N_D(c) = B(c), the union of the c-star leaves. Every other w ∈ N(c) has c ∈ B_w, and is carried by the
tree gate x_c ∨ z of the w-star, whose z has a singleton. Also k_Z >= 1. Apply (c). ∎
The Petersen circuit of t7 §2 (size Phi, where T2-LT fails) has AND-depth 1. Its paid A options are exactly its six
star centres, as SL predicts. SL contains Theorem A(A2) of star.md, for reduced circuits.

**Checks (DATA, `a8.py`, `a11.py`).** Bound (a): 0 violations on all A options of the pools. Step (b): 0 violations
in 113,326 instances where its hypotheses hold. The certificate of (c) holds at some vertex in **18,378 of 18,535**
pool circuits, and every certified option is in fact LD-paid (58,057/58,057). The weaker version without OR
readers (DV: all readers of x_c in Z, I = variables only) certifies 15,236 circuits.

## 3. What dead gates do not give (REFUTED / DATA)

* **T2-LD is REFUTED.** T2-LD says: some A/N/B option has LD >= need. Counterexample (`a3.py`): the net,
  size 10 = Phi + 2, from t3 pool_net:
  `6=0|3 7=4|5 8=6&7 9=5|8 10=2|8 11=1|4 12=11&9 13=3|12 14=10&13 15=14|12` (max LD − need = −1).
  Every option that LR pays here (B(0), N(1), N(2), B(2), B(4)) is paid by the **Top merge**. One example is
  B(0): K = {6}, Cp = {8}, no dead gates, while 14 and 15 compute the same function under rho. Three more such net
  circuits are in pool_net. On the t7 pools T2-LD holds on 18,535/18,535 circuits, and on t3 pool_ML + pool_net it
  holds on 221,870/221,874; on the t2 cache (70,452) it never fails. So Top is rarely needed, but it is needed. Any structural proof must count both dead
  gates and output absorption.
* **Star centres in STAR-ALL circuits: REFUTED.** On the t7 pools some star centre is LD-paid in all 10,081
  STAR-ALL circuits, but not at every centre. In the t2 cache it fails 3 times out of 57,429. The smallest failure in the cache is
  [03,04,14,24,34,35] at size 8 = Phi+1, `6=0|2 7=0|5 8=4&6 9=3|8 10=1|9 11=7&9 12=4|11 13=10&12` (centres 3 and 4).
  The fuzzer (`fuzz8.py SAc`) also breaks it on triangle-free graphs: C5{012} at size 14 = Phi+3 and n8 at size
  16 = Phi+3 (camp.log).
* **"k_or(c) = 0 implies A(c) LD-paid", REFUTED.** Smallest counterexample: net, Phi+1,
  `6=3&2 7=6|5 8=7|3 9=0|8 10=4&9 11=3|1 12=11&8 13=12&7 14=10|13`, c = 4, LD = 3 < 4. The cause is that the shared
  gate 8 enters Z_4 as one I-arc while it carries {3, 5}. This is exactly pen(4) = 1 in DV2.
* Averaging A + B at one vertex (LD_A(c) + LD_B(c) >= need_A + need_B for some c) fails in 7,233 pool circuits,
  including both t7 counterexamples.

**Fuzzing (`camp.log`, 90 s per graph, size <= Phi+3).**
* T2-LD: only the net fails (size 10 = Phi+2, `6=3|1 7=3|5 8=6&7 9=5|2 10=9&8 11=0|10 12=4|10 13=11&12 14=7&12 15=14|13`).
  The minimum number of LD-paid options is 2 on Petersen, Petersen-v, sun5 and C5{012}, 3 on constrS7, 4 on
  2tri+bridge, and 6 on n8 and C7. T2-LD also holds on all 70,452 circuits of the t2 cache (`a15.py`).
* Corollary SL (sanity check): 0 failures on 9 graphs. The minimum number of paid centres found was 3 to 6.

## 3b. Does a DV2-certified vertex always exist? (DATA / REFUTED)

* **For arbitrary reduced circuits: REFUTED, on every graph tried** (`camp2.log`, size <= Phi+3). Examples: net at
  Phi+2, C7 at Phi+3, P6 at Phi+2 (`6=0|2 7=3|1 8=2|1 9=7&8 10=9&6 11=4|10 12=3|5 13=11&12 14=10&13`), and also
  C5{012}, C6{01}, sun5, Petersen, Petersen-v, n8 and 2tri+bridge. So DV2 is a sufficient local condition. It is not
  a complete strategy: forests and cycles still need B options (t2 Leaf and Cycle Lemmas).
* **Size <= Phi, triangle-free: no failure.** In the t2 cache, every triangle-free circuit of size Phi (5,053 of them,
  all C4-free triangle-free graphs on <= 7 vertices) has a certified vertex. Fuzzing at size <= Phi (`camp3.log`, K = 0,
  120 s per graph) found no failure on 11 graphs, including Petersen, Petersen-v, sun5, C5{012}, C6{01}, n8, C7 and
  P6. The minimum number of certified vertices there is 3 to 6.
* **Size Phi with triangles: REFUTED.** 477 of the 16,168 size-Phi triangle circuits in the cache have no certified
  vertex. The smallest is the bull [01,02,04,12,23], `5=0|2 6=0|3 7=2|4 8=6&7 9=1|8 10=5&9`, where gate 8 is
  junk-carrying. Another is the net, `6=2|4 7=4|3 8=6&7 9=1|7 10=0|3 11=10&8 12=5|11 13=12&9` (fuzzer).
* **Size Phi+1, triangle-free: REFUTED.** The smallest is P4 [01,03,12],
  `4=0|1 5=0|2 6=1|3 7=5&6 8=4&7` (a C4-type rectangle followed by a killer). Next: C5{012} at size 12, Petersen and
  others in the pools.

Reading. For girth >= 5, "every reduced circuit of size <= Phi has a DV2-certified vertex" survives everything
tested. With R-TOT-F it would close girth >= 5, since a minimal counterexample has size Phi − 1 and a certified vertex
makes A(c) good. **Caveat (as in t7):** at size <= Phi − 1 the statement is vacuous if the claim is true. It is a
proof target only if the proof derives the certificate from the size bound, for example by showing that a
circuit with no certified vertex wastes a gate.

## 4. Where the remaining 157 circuits fail (DATA, `a12.py`, `a13.py`)

117 of them are Petersen, 25 constrS7, 8 net and 7 partial suns. In every one some option is LR-paid: A in 153, N in
157, B in 35. At the A options that are really paid but not certified, the cause is:
* pen >= 1 in 225 cases: a shared gate y carries two neighbours of c into Z_c, and it is paid back by the excess
  Σ(r − 1) or by extra arcs;
* k_Z = 0 in 19 cases: all readers of x_c are live ORs, and δ_c = 1;
* the single-carrier hypothesis fails in 4 cases, and the singleton hypothesis in 2.

## 5. Exact gap

DV2 reduces "A(c) is paid" to a single local defect: **pen(c) > k_Z − δ_c**. A live gate y with
|S_c(y) ∩ N(c)| >= 2 enters the dead region of c as one arc. In a C4-free graph two neighbours of c have no
common neighbour other than c. So the live use of y is either junk, or a second gate creating the edges cw_1 and cw_2.
That is a redundancy. It should make some other option pay, but no such transfer is proved. To prove T2-LR
for a graph class one needs: for every reduced C, either some vertex has pen(c) <= k_Z − δ_c and the carrier
hypotheses hold, or some B/N option (or a Top merge) pays. The fuzzing in §3 shows that the first alternative alone
does not hold for every circuit. For girth >= 5 it holds on all data at size <= Phi (§3b).

Concretely, for girth >= 5 the open step is: **(DV2-Phi) if no vertex of C is DV2-certified, then size(C) >= Phi + 1.**
The uncertified examples inspected by hand (P4, bull, net) contain either a rectangle followed by a killer (the P4 shape) or a shared gate y
that carries two neighbours of some c (pen >= 1) and is used live a second time. Both are redundancies, and one
would hope to charge each to a gate saved elsewhere. No such charging is proved.
