# L-meta-612, route T7: what Top_X really is, and the fall of T2-LT

Status: **NOT closed. T2-LT is REFUTED** (Petersen, size exactly Phi = 20, a star circuit; and
Petersen-v, size Phi+1). No graph-term lower bound on |Top_X| exists. The quantity that survives is
the **syntactic cascade** (constants, copies and *dead* gates, i.e. rule R) plus the Top merge. That is
still a structural count, so it is not affected by the min-cex caveat.

Labels: PROVED, DATA, REFUTED (explicit circuit, checked on all 2^n inputs). Notation is that of t2/t5:
K_rho = gates that are constant under rho, Cp_rho = non-constant gates with an input in K ∪ x_rho, and
Top_rho = {q : q|rho = out|rho}. LO = |K ∪ Cp|, LT = |K ∪ Cp ∪ Top| - 1,
LR = size(C) - |ruleR(C|rho)| + (#Top gates among the rule-R survivors - 1).

## 1. Measurements: what Top consists of (DATA)

Pools: t3 locpool plus t5 pool and pool2 (18,533 circuits), and a 92k sample of the t2 cache (`an1.py`,
`an2.py`, `t5/t5an4.py`).
* For A/N options, K = D_X and Cp = ∂D_X, so LO is exactly t2's first-order bound. For B, LO = |R_v| + |∂R_v|.
  So **T2-LT = T2-LO + Top**.
* LO alone already pays some option in 18,529 of 18,533 pool circuits and in 91,958 of 91,959 cache
  circuits. **Top is needed in only 5 circuits**: 4 Petersen circuits from pool2 (size Phi+2, paid only
  by N(8)) and the net Phi+1 circuit of t2. In each of them Top adds exactly +1. Each time the gain is
  an **absorption at the output**: out = u ∘ w with u|rho and w|rho comparable. For example
  31 = 30|22 with 22|rho <= 30|rho, or, on the net, 14 = 12&13 with 12|rho = 11 <= 13|rho. The extra Top
  gates are OR/AND gates sandwiched between f_H and f_G.
* |Top| = 1 is the typical value: A 92%, B 93%, N 51% of (circuit, option) pairs.
  In **5,372 circuits no option has any Top contribution** (|Top \ (K∪Cp)| <= 1 for every option). The
  smallest such circuit has size Phi, on C5{012}:
  `8=3|7 9=6&1 10=1|8 11=2&10 12=5|1 13=0|9 14=13&12 15=11|14 16=3|13 17=4&16 18=17|15`.

**Proposition T0 (PROVED, trivial).** Top_X = {q : q >= f_H and q|rho <= f_H}, and it contains every
gate q with f_H <= q <= f_G. All gates of Top_X except the earliest are eliminated, and the earliest
is not in D_X ∪ ∂D_X (t5 Lemma ST).
**REFUTED: any lower bound |Top_X \ (K∪Cp)| >= h(G,X) with h >= 2 for some option X.** The C5{012}
circuit above is a counterexample at size Phi. So the Top term is a circuit-shape artefact (an
absorption where the OR-tree is a chain) and cannot carry a graph-theoretic count. The "output cone"
and "functions f_H ∨ (X-stuff)" ideas both reduce to this set. A balanced top OR-tree that splits
E(H) between its two sides makes Top = {out} for that X.

## 2. T2-LT is REFUTED (adversarial fuzzing)

`fuzzLT.py` hill-climbs on the number of LT-paid options (size <= Phi+3, 120 s per graph, one process,
`camp.log`). On 17 graphs the minimum number of LT-paid options stays >= 1: net 1, psun C5{012}/C6{01}/C6{03} 2,
sun5 2, C6+2p 2, constrS7 3, n8 3, C7ch 3, 2C5e 4, bull 4, and 5-8 on the others. It fails on 2 graphs (`chk.py`):
* **Petersen, size 20 = Phi**. This is the fuzzer's *starting* star circuit:
  `10=1|5 11=10|4 12=0&11 13=3|7 14=1|13 15=2&14 16=8|1 17=16|9 18=6&17 19=8|4 20=3&19 21=4|7 22=9&21 23=7|8 24=5&23 25=18|20 26=25|12 27=26|24 28=15|22 29=28|27`.
  max(LT - need) = -1. The truly good options are A(0), A(2), A(3), A(5), A(6), A(9) (elim 4 = need, LT 2-3),
  N(1), N(4), N(7), N(8) and B(1).
* **Petersen-v, size 17 = Phi+1**:
  `9=4|2 10=4|1 11=10|8 12=8|2 13=3&12 14=7|8 15=14|0 16=5&15 17=6&11 18=7&9 19=16|18 20=1|19 21=19|13 22=2|0 23=20&22 24=23|17 25=21|24`.
  max(LT - need) = -1, but A(3), A(5), A(6), A(7) and several N and B options are truly good.

Why (PROVED for the mechanism): in a star gate S_c = x_c ∧ (x_a ∨ x_b ∨ ...), A(c) zeroes S_c, makes
its OR reader a copy, and makes the inner OR chain of the star (deg c - 1 gates) **dead**, not constant
and not a copy. So for a star circuit, need_A(c) = deg c + δ_c is met only after the dead gates are counted.
LO and LT see 2 to 3 of the 4 gates. Any first-order + Top statement therefore already fails on Petersen
at size Phi. So t5's 18,533/18,533 was a property of non-adversarial pools.

## 3. The surviving structural statement: T2-LR (DATA)

**T2-LR.** For every reduced C there is an A, N or B option with LR(rho) >= need(rho).
LR counts only structure: syntactic constant propagation, copies, dead gates (t2 rule R) and the Top
merge. Each counted gate is eliminated (PROVED, t2 §0 + Lemma ST(a)), so **T2-LR + R-TOT-F implies the
claim**. Moreover it never asks whether the claim holds on smaller graphs, so the min-cex caveat does not apply.
* DATA: 18,535/18,535 (all pools plus both T2-LT counterexamples), `an5.py`.
* Adversarial fuzzing against LR: no counterexample (§5).

## 4. Classes where the structural statements are PROVED

* **T2-LO (hence T2-LT, T2-LR) holds for every reduced circuit of every C4-free graph whose components
  are trees or cycles.** This is PROVED: t2's Leaf Lemma and Cycle Lemma use only the first-order sets
  D_v ∪ ∂D_v and R_v ∪ ∂R_v, and the case analysis of Theorems F1/F2 picks such an option.
* Full suns and their unions with bare cycles: t4 Lemma R2 is first-order, but it uses the option
  x_P := 1, which is outside the A/N/B family.
* Pseudoforests, subcubic graphs and C4-free graphs in general: not proved. T2-LT is false already on Petersen
  (cubic, girth 5), so **no proof of T2-LT can exist for subcubic or for all C4-free graphs**. For pseudoforests
  T2-LT survived the fuzzing (min 2 paid options on C5{012}, C6{01}, C6{03}, sun5, C6+2p), but at bad leaves
  "N(w) paid by LT" holds only in 14,596 of 17,823 cases (true elim: all of them). So the leaf payments of t3 also need dead gates.

## 5. LR fuzzing results

`fuzzLT.py ... LR` (size <= Phi+3, 120 s per graph, `camp2.log`). **No counterexample.** Minimum number
of LR-paid options: Petersen 2, Petersen-v 2, net 2, n8 2, sun5 3, C5{012} 3, C6{01} 4, C7ch 5,
constrS7 6, 2C5e 7. Hence T2-LR is DATA (survives adversarial search on 10 graphs, including the two graphs
where T2-LT fails). By comparison, the same fuzzer refutes T2-LT on Petersen at the start circuit.

**Exact gap after T7.** The true payments use **dead gates**: the inner OR chains of stars whose AND
gate became 0. A structural proof therefore has to bound |dead_rho|, and not |Top_X|. The natural next
lemma is a dead-gate version of the Reader Lemma: under x_c := 0, every gate whose readers all lie in
D_c ∪ dead is dead. For a star gate x_c ∧ y with y read only inside the star, this gives
|dead| >= (size of y's private cone). No such lemma is proved here.
